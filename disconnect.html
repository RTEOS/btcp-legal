Disconnecting from QuickBooks

How to revoke this application's access at any time.

Disconnecting takes effect immediately. Once revoked, the application can no longer read any data from QuickBooks Online.

To disconnect

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online as an administrator.
  2. Go to Settings → Apps, or open the Apps menu and choose My Apps.
  3. Find BTCP QuickBooks Integration in the list of connected apps.
  4. Choose Disconnect and confirm.

What happens to data already retrieved

Data previously retrieved for reconciliation is held on systems controlled by BTCP and is retained only as long as needed for the analysis it was retrieved for. To request deletion of retrieved data, contact us at the address below.

Reconnecting later

See Connect. Disconnecting is fully reversible.
