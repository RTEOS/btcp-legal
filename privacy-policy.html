Privacy Policy — BTCP QuickBooks Integration

Effective date: 15 September 2026

Applies to: the internal QuickBooks Online integration operated by Brandenberger Tree Care Professionals ("BTCP", "we", "us").

1. What this application is

This is a private, internal application. It is used solely by Brandenberger Tree Care

Professionals to read and reconcile our own accounting records in QuickBooks Online.

It is not offered to, sold to, or installed by any other company or individual. There are no

external users. The only QuickBooks company it connects to is our own.

2. What data it accesses

The application connects to one QuickBooks Online company file — ours — and reads accounting

records, including invoices, income accounts, customers, and financial reports.

It accesses this data to reconcile our invoicing against our field-operations system and to detect

bookkeeping errors, such as duplicate or orphaned transactions.

3. Whose data this is

All data accessed is our own business's accounting data. It may include the names, contact

details and transaction amounts of our customers, because those appear in our own invoices.

We do not access any other company's QuickBooks data, and the application is technically incapable

of doing so — it is authorized against a single company file.

4. How the data is used

The data is used only for:

  • reconciling our accounting records against our own operational records;
  • detecting and correcting bookkeeping errors in our own books;
  • internal financial reporting and analysis.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, license or trade any data accessed through this application.
  • We do not share it with third parties for marketing or advertising.
  • We do not use it to build products or services for anyone outside BTCP.
  • We do not use it for automated decisions affecting any individual.

6. Storage and retention

Data retrieved through the integration is held only as long as needed for the reconciliation or

analysis being performed, and is stored on systems controlled by BTCP. Access credentials are held

in restricted storage, are not shared, and are not committed to any code repository.

7. Security

  • Access is via Intuit's OAuth 2.0. We do not store QuickBooks usernames or passwords.
  • Credentials are stored with restricted file permissions and excluded from version control.
  • Access tokens are short-lived and refreshed automatically.
  • Write access is disabled by default and requires an explicit, recorded, time-limited

authorization before any change can be made to accounting records.

8. Revoking access

Because this application serves only BTCP, access can be revoked at any time from within our own

QuickBooks Online account, under Apps → Manage. Revoking access immediately ends the

application's ability to read any data.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this address with an updated

effective date.
